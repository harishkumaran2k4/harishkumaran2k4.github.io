## The Magic of Coffee

Most engineers swear by coffee. It fuels long nights of debugging and designing complex circuits.

### Coffee & Productivity

|Time of Day|Best Type of Coffee|
|---|---|
|Morning|Black Coffee|
|Afternoon|Espresso|
|Evening|Decaf Latte|

> "Give me enough coffee, and I will build you a processor." – Anonymous Engineer

## The Science Behind It

Caffeine blocks adenosine receptors, keeping you alert and focused.

## Conclusion

Next time you hit a debugging roadblock, grab a cup of coffee!
